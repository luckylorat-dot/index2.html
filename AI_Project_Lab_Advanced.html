General performance anchors

85–100: strong, consistent evidence; 70–84: secure evidence with minor gaps; 50–69: partial or uneven evidence; 0–49: limited evidence.

The composite score is an author-developed descriptive index of project-work quality, not a validated measurement model.

Data Processing & Assessment Comparability

Scoring and data processing

Each of the seven indicators is scored on a 0–100 scale. The composite score is the arithmetic mean of the seven equally weighted indicators. Equal weighting is used as a transparent descriptive procedure and should not be interpreted as a validated measurement model. Student self-assessment and peer feedback are supplementary evidence rather than independent evidence of improved performance.

Data limitations

Only group-level results are available for the present report. Individual scores, measures of variability and evidence of baseline group comparability were not preserved. Statistical significance, effect sizes and causal effects should therefore not be calculated from these aggregate data. The available data do not permit independent reconstruction of the original scoring procedure.

Assessment comparability

All three groups were intended to be assessed using the same seven indicators. However, G3 used structured process logs that directly documented several assessed characteristics, particularly critical thinking and learner autonomy. Equivalent process-recording forms for G1 and G2 are not available in the present evidence set. This may have given G3 an advantage in demonstrating these indicators.

Requirement for replication

In a repeated study, all groups should have equal opportunities to demonstrate the assessed skills. Only the AI-support condition under investigation should differ. Individual ratings, assessor comments and anonymised raw data should be retained.
